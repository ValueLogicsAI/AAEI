# Drafts — not part of AAEI v1.2

Nothing in this folder is submitted, tested, or covered by the AAEI v1.2
conformance suite.

**AAEI v1.2 is the twelve-field schema** at `schemas/aaei_schema_v1_2.json`.
That is what the OMG submission paper and the Letters of Intent to the Business
Architecture TF, the AI TF and the MDA Working Group all specify.

This folder holds early field design for two standards named in the MDA WG letter
as future submissions:

- **LDR** — Logic Density Ratio
- **VLH** — Value Logic Hash

`ldr_vlh_extended_profile_draft.json` was briefly published as `schemas/aaei_v1_2.json`,
where it conflicted with the AAEI v1.2 schema. It is kept here for the field design.
